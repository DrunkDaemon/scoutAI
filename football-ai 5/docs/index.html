ScoutAI

A field guide to expected goals, built from real shots and a machine-learning model that runs right here in your browser.

Where you shoot from changes everything.

Expected goals (xG) is the chance that a shot becomes a goal. Move the shot, add defenders, change the keeper, and watch the model react.

Shots0Goals0Expected0.0

Click or drag on the pitch to move the shot. Then take it twenty times: your goals will land close to your expected total. That is the whole idea of xG.

    How good is the model?

    Tested on matches it had never seen. The scale starts at 0.5, which is what a coin flip scores.

    What the model pays attention to

    1. The data. Real shots with the positions of every defender and the goalkeeper at the moment of the shot, from StatsBomb’s open data.

    2. The features. Distance, the angle of the goal as the shooter sees it, defenders in the shooting lane, keeper position, body part and situation.

    3. The model. Gradient boosting, tuned with grouped cross-validation so shots from one match never appear in both training and testing.

    4. The website. The 300 trained trees are exported to JavaScript, so there is no server. Every number you see is computed on your device.
